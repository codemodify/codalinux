CodaLinux pointer and keyboard defaults
======================================

Keyboard layout: us (Bozeman / US default — not prompted at install)
Modifier: Super (Windows key)

  Super+Return     Terminal (foot)
  Super+Space      App launcher (AGS)
  Super+D          App launcher (AGS)
  Super+,          Control center (AGS settings)
  Super+V          Clipboard history (AGS)
  Super+E          Files (Thunar)
  Super+F          Firefox
  Super+L          Lock
  Super+Q          Close window
  Super+1..0       Workspaces
  Volume / media   wpctl + playerctl

Touchpad (laptops):
  natural scroll   on
  tap to click     on

To change compositor input, edit:
  ~/.config/hypr/hyprland.lua

GTK fonts, icons, and cursors:
  Super+,  →  Appearance  (nwg-look)

Wi-Fi uses iwd (impala). Bluetooth, audio, webcam, and appearance
are official apps opened from the AGS control center.
